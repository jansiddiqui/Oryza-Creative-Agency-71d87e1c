# Image prompts

Generated by `npm run images:prompts` from `src/data/image-slots.ts` and `src/data/image-scenes.ts` — do not edit by hand.

## How to use

Copy a prompt → paste into ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney → generate 3–4 versions → pick the most natural one → upload it in **/admin/images** (guide: `docs/admin-images.md`). Nothing needs to be added to the prompt.

- **Illustrative** slots may use AI-generated images as decorative or concept scenes only.
- **Real-only** slots (work, team, clients, results, our office) take a **real photo or nothing**. No prompt is given for them.
- Share images (OG) are made automatically from their page header.

## Human review checklist (before approving)

- [ ] **Hands and fingers look natural** — Five fingers each, natural joints, nothing fused or floating.
- [ ] **Faces look natural** — Natural skin and eyes; no identifiable children or patients; nobody looks like a specific real person.
- [ ] **No text, watermarks or logos** — Zoom in on signs, screens, papers and labels — no lettering, no watermark, no brand marks.
- [ ] **The setting is believably Indian** — Clothing, buildings, vehicles, signage shapes and light feel like a real North Indian city.
- [ ] **Nothing misleading** — It is not shown as Oryza's client work, team, office or results.
- [ ] **Matches the brand mood** — Calm, natural, muted warm tones that sit with deep green #13201C and gold #B9811A; no neon or heavy saturation.
